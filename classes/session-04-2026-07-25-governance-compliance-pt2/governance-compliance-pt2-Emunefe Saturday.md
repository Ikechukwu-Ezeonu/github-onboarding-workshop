# My Notes — Emunefe Saturday 


Key Concepts I Learned

Understood the importance of data classification and how sensitivity labels (such as Public, General, Confidential, and Highly Confidential) help organizations protect information and meet compliance requirements.

Learned how Microsoft Purview Information Protection uses sensitivity labels to classify and secure sensitive data across Microsoft 365 services.

Explored Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and how they prevent accidental or intentional sharing of sensitive information through email, Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive.

Learned how DLP policies use sensitive information types, conditions, and actions to detect and protect confidential data.

Gained an understanding of Insider Risk Management in Microsoft Purview and how it identifies risky user activities such as unusual file downloads, data exfiltration, or policy violations.

Learned how governance, compliance, and data protection work together to reduce organizational risk and satisfy regulatory requirements.

Connected these concepts to real Microsoft SC-500 certification scenarios involving data protection, compliance, and insider threat detection.
